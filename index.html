Care that begins at the cuticle.

Diana specializes in the Russian dry manicure: a waterless technique where the cuticle is refined with a fine e-file instead of being soaked and clipped. The result is a clean, even line around the nail that grows out gracefully.

Every set is built to last — builder gel to strengthen natural nails, and extensions sculpted without tips, glue or acrylic.
